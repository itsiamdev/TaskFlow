What is TaskFlow?

TaskFlow is a concept for a simple productivity workspace designed to help you organize tasks, clarify priorities, and keep track of your day.

Can I organize tasks by priority?

The design includes visual high, medium, and low priority labels. This static demo shows how that feature could look in a finished product.

Can I manage multiple projects?

The interface mockup includes project categories to demonstrate how tasks could be grouped by area or project.

Does TaskFlow work on mobile devices?

This landing page is responsive and adapts to phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Can I track my progress?

Progress indicators and charts are included as visual examples in this frontend concept.

Is TaskFlow free to use?

This is a visual demo, not a launched service. Pricing and availability would be defined when the product is built.
